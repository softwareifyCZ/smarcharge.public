Privacy Policy

Last updated: 17 November 2026

1. Who we are

SmarCharge is operated by SMAR s.r.o., Škroupova 1397/48, 430 01 Chomutov, Company ID 24308749 (the “controller”). The app is developed and technically maintained by Softwareify on behalf of SMAR. Contact: developer@smar.cz.

2. What SmarCharge does

The app lets you view EV chargers assigned to your account, start and stop charging sessions, review your charging history and manage RFID/NFC tags that authorise charging.

3. Data we process

DataPurposeWhere

The app also downloads a public configuration file from Apple iCloud. No personal data is sent to iCloud.

We do not track you, do not use advertising identifiers, do not sell data and use no third-party analytics or advertising tools.

4. NFC

NFC is used only when you choose to scan or program a tag. The tag identifier is sent to the backend only to authorise charging.

5. Legal basis

We process the data to perform the contract for using the charging service (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR) and, for accounting records of charging sessions, to comply with legal obligations (Art. 6(1)(c) GDPR).

6. Sharing

Data is shared only with our infrastructure and development providers acting as processors — Amazon Web Services (hosting) and Softwareify (app development and IT operations) — and with the operator of the charger you use, where needed to provide and bill the charging service. We do not transfer data to other parties.

7. Retention

We keep your account data while your account exists. Charging session records may be kept for up to 10 years as required by accounting law; after account deletion they are kept in anonymised form.

8. Your rights and account deletion

You have the right to access, correct, delete and export your data, and to object to or restrict processing. To exercise any right, including deleting your account, write to developer@smar.cz from your account e-mail. We will respond within 30 days.

You may also lodge a complaint with the Czech Office for Personal Data Protection (uoou.gov.cz).

9. Changes

We will publish any changes on this page and update the date above.

NFC se používá jen tehdy, když sami zvolíte načtení nebo naprogramování tagu. Identifikátor tagu se odesílá na backend pouze kvůli autorizaci nabíjení.
